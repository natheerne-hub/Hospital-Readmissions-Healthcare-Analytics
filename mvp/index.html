Design principle

No unexplained score appears in the interface. Each result must trace to a defined source, transformation, model version, and interpretation rule.

Data-separation principle

CMS hospital records and patient encounters are not concatenated simply to increase row count. Datasets are combined only after a defensible harmonization study.
