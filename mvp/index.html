Design principle

No unexplained score appears in the interface. Each KPI must trace back to a defined source field, transformation, denominator, and interpretation rule.

Safety principle

The public MVP is decision-support analytics only. It must not present itself as a diagnostic device or as a validated individual clinical prediction system until appropriate validation is completed.
